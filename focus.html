Dry crops are the most fragmented supply system in agriculture

Shunah builds the infrastructure layer for dry crop supply — organizing fragmented production into structured, tradable flow through aggregation, quality standardization, and coordinated delivery.

Fragmented Supply Base — Millions of small-scale producers create a highly fragmented and unstructured supply layer.

Unstructured Category — Dry crops dominate production — yet remain difficult to aggregate at scale.

System at Scale — A multi-billion-ton supply system that cannot be efficiently traded without infrastructure.

Disconnected Flow — Less than 2% of supply is connected to structured demand affecting the whole cylce.
